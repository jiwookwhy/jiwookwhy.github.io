I'm a recent Finance PhD graduate from Duke University's Fuqua School of Business and a Quantitative Finance Analyst (Vice President) at Bank of America, where I develop deposit forecasting models. My research studies how risk is priced in options markets, with a focus on the factor structure of short-maturity options, risk premia around macroeconomic announcements across asset classes, and the joint calibration and hedging of option pricing models.
