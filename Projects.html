  1. jointcal: Option Pricing, Calibration, and Hedging

    Python

    jointcal calibrates option pricing models to the implied volatility surface and the variance term structure jointly, as in my paper Joint Calibration of the Volatility Surface and Variance Term Structure. It also prices options, computes Greeks, and sets hedges.

    • Models: Bates, Heston, Merton, and Black–Scholes are built in. A new model needs only its parameters and characteristic function; the example notebook adds the double Heston model in a few lines.
    • Calibration: one weight trades off the fit to the volatility surface against the fit to the variance term structure. Restricted fits can pin parameters such as the spot variance or the jump-size law.
    • Pricing and Greeks: COS Fourier pricing, with delta, gamma, vega, vanna, volga, and theta.
    • Hedging: Black–Scholes and model deltas, and a quadratic hedge that minimizes risk-neutral variance, jumps included, using the underlying and any options, with Tikhonov regularization.
    • A test suite, and an example notebook that walks through one trading day from start to finish.
  2. 0DTE Option Pricing Calibrator

    NumPy, SciPy

    A Python implementation of the 0DTE option pricing model of Bandi, Fusari, and Renò (forthcoming, Journal of Finance): option pricing, daily calibration with an optional variance term structure penalty, the model's variance term structure, and simulation. Includes the model's parameters fitted to S&P 500 options from 1996 to 2025, about 19,500 fits in all.

  3. VentML: ICU Decision Support

    Python, Q-learning

    A Q-learning decision-support prototype that uses MIMIC-III electronic health records to jointly recommend ventilator weaning decisions and sedation levels. Graduate computer science capstone with collaborators from Duke's engineering and medical schools.
