  1. jointcal: Option Pricing, Calibration, and Hedging

    Python

    jointcal fits option pricing models to the implied volatility surface and the variance term structure jointly, and tests how the fitted models hedge. It is the companion library to Joint Calibration of the Volatility Surface and Variance Term Structure.

    • Joint calibration of implied volatility surfaces and variance term structures for the Bates, Heston, Merton, and Black–Scholes models.
    • Modular design: adding a model requires only its characteristic function and parameter specification.
    • Vectorized COS Fourier pricing and Greeks.
    • Jump-aware quadratic hedging with multiple options and Tikhonov regularization.
  2. Static Hedging with Neural Networks

    Python, TensorFlow

    Two feed-forward neural networks for static hedging of index options: a policy network picks three nearby contracts on the volatility surface, and an evaluation network sets the portfolio weights that best hedge the target option over a chosen holding period. Trained on SPX and VIX options, with pre-trained models included.

  3. VentML: ICU Decision Support

    Python, Q-learning

    A Q-learning decision-support prototype that uses MIMIC-III electronic health records to jointly recommend ventilator weaning decisions and sedation levels. Graduate computer science capstone with collaborators from Duke's engineering and medical schools.
