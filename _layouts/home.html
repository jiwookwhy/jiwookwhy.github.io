  • Research

    My working papers and publications. Most of my research is about how risk gets priced in options markets: the factor structure of very short-dated options (which won Best Paper at APAD 2025), risk premia around macroeconomic announcements across equities, rates, currencies, and commodities, and how to jointly calibrate and hedge option pricing models.

  • Projects

    Code that goes with my research, including jointcal, a Python library for option pricing, calibration, and hedging, and a neural network approach to static hedging. There's also an ICU decision-support tool built with reinforcement learning from my computer science coursework.

  • Teaching

    Courses I helped teach at Fuqua for the Daytime MBA and Master of Quantitative Management (MQM) programs, covering derivatives, fixed income, risk management, and finance data analytics.
